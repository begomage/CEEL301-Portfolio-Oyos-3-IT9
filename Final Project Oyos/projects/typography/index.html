Project Overview

A typography-focused poster study drawing visual inspiration from the Devil May Cry game series.

Objective

Explore expressive typography and graphic composition through a poster inspired by the visual world of Devil May Cry.

Approach

The poster pairs high-contrast red lettering with colorful typographic details and a dark visual treatment to create a dramatic, type-led composition.

Features

  • Expressive, type-led poster composition
  • High-contrast red lettering
  • Color and type details inspired by the game's visual style
