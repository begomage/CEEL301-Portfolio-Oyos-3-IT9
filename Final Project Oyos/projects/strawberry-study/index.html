Strawberry Study

A hand-drawn fruit illustration exploring colored-pencil texture, shape, and natural color.

← Back to project albums

Project Overview

This drawing study depicts a strawberry with its leafy top, using colored pencil on paper. The exercise focuses on observing the fruit's form and translating its red and green colors into a hand-rendered illustration.

Objective

Practice representing a familiar natural subject through careful drawing and colored-pencil rendering.

Approach

The strawberry's silhouette, seeds, and leaves are drawn by hand, then built up with red and green pencil color and visible strokes to suggest surface and form.

Features

  • Hand-drawn strawberry illustration
  • Colored-pencil rendering
  • Seed and leaf details
