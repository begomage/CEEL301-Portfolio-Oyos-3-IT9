Project Overview

A visual identity project for Midnight Overture, featuring a badge emblem and complementary icon and wordmark variations.

Objective

Develop a recognizable visual identity for Midnight Overture that can be presented in both emblematic and compact logo formats.

Approach

A blue rose and dark crest form the central badge, with the identity adapted into icon, vertical, and stacked horizontal logo variations.

Features

  • Badge emblem
  • Icon logo
  • Vertical logo
  • Stacked horizontal logo
